Privacy Policy

Effective 28 September 2026.

Iterum is a private automation system run by taskAIgnol LLC for its own use. It is not offered to the public.

  • Who uses it. Only taskAIgnol LLC's own Google account (taskaignol@gmail.com).
  • What it reads. The Google data that account allows on Google's consent screen (for example Gmail, Drive, Calendar and Contacts), only to carry out the account owner's own tasks.
  • Where it goes. Data stays on the LLC's own computers. It is not sold, not shared, not used for advertising, and not used to train general AI models.
  • Keeping it safe. The Google permission is stored in one locked place, and every use is logged.
  • Stopping it. The account owner can remove Iterum's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Google data. Iterum's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
